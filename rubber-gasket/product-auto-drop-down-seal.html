Auto Drop Down Seal

Mechanical seals that stay retracted while a door swings, then drop automatically to close the threshold gap — built for fire-rated and acoustic doors.

Concealed drop down seal

Mortised into a housing routed along the door's bottom edge for a fully hidden finish — sized for 13x28mm door bottom channels.

HousingAluminium, mortised
Size13x28mm
Typical useFire-rated & acoustic doors

External drop down seal

Surface-mounted to the face of the door where routing isn't possible — a straightforward retrofit for existing doors, sized for 14x43mm channels.

HousingAluminium, surface-mounted
Size14x43mm
Typical useRetrofit & renovation projects

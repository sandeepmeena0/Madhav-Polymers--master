Door Seals

Bottom seals and glass door seals that close the gap at floor and frame level — for a quieter, more energy-efficient room.

Door bottom seal

A brush or fin seal fitted to the base of a door leaf that blocks drafts, dust and insects while the door swings freely.

MaterialPVC / brush pile
FitSurface or recessed mount
Typical useInterior & exterior doors

Glass door seal

Edge and bottom seals sized for frameless and semi-frameless glass doors, keeping the panel cushioned and the gap sealed.

MaterialEPDM / PVC
FitClip-on edge profile
Typical useFrameless glass doors

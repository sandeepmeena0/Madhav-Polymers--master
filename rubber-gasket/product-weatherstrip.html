Product family

Weatherstrip

Pile weatherstrips that block drafts, dust and noise at door and window frames — available siliconized or non-siliconized, cut to your channel width.

Non-siliconized weatherstrip

Our general-purpose pile weatherstrip for standard door and window channels — a dependable, economical draft seal for everyday fabrication work.

Pile materialPolypropylene
BackingRigid PP fin
Typical useAluminium & UPVC frames

Siliconized fin seal weatherstrip

A fin-seal design with a silicone-treated pile for smoother glide and stronger resistance to moisture and UV exposure — suited to higher-traffic openings.

Pile materialSiliconized polypropylene
DesignFin seal, dual-row pile
Typical useSliding windows & patio doors

Siliconized weatherstrip

Full-length silicone treatment for maximum durability in demanding climates — our recommended option where the seal will see years of continuous opening and closing.

Pile materialSiliconized polypropylene
BackingFlexible fin, wool-pile core
Typical usePremium window & door systems

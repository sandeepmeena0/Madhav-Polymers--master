PVC Seals & Profiles

Flexible PVC profile seals for interior fittings — from kitchen shutters to glass shower enclosures.

Kitchen & wardrobe profile seal

PVC edge gaskets sized for 45mm and 50mm kitchen profiles, plus curve and 20x20mm wardrobe profiles — a clean finish that resists moisture and staining.

MaterialFlexible PVC
Sizes45mm, 50mm, 20x20mm profiles
Typical useModular kitchens & wardrobes

Office partition seal

PVC centre and side seals that close the gap between partition panels while allowing them to be reconfigured without damage.

MaterialFlexible PVC
ProfilesCentre seal, side seal "h"
